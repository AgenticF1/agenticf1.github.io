Pace what-if explorer

Clean-air pace is what's left of a driver's lap time after the model subtracts the estimated effects of dirty air, tyre degradation, fuel and track evolution, and temperature. Those effects are estimates. Drag any of them and every driver's derived pace and rank moves — each by how much of that condition they actually ran.
