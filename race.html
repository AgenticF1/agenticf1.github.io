Fitted race pace distribution

The same fitting can be run several ways — treating traffic, tyre wear and the reference field a little differently each time. The bar is the full range a driver's fitted lap time moves across those runs; the dot is the version shown above. A short bar means the answer is settled; a long one means their pace depends on how you account for traffic.

    Section 04

    How each effect was fitted

    Every lap is heavier on fuel, older on tyres or closer to another car than the reference lap the pace figure is quoted at. Each panel strips out everything except one of those effects and shows what is left: the cloud is the leftover lap time, the line is the fitted effect the model removes, and the figure at the top of each panel is how big that effect was. The overtake-mode panel is a before/after instead — the spread of leftover lap time with a car more than a second up the road versus within a second of it, the black bar marking each average.
