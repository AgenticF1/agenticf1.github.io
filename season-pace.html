2026 season

Race pace, race by race

Every 2026 Grand Prix and Sprint, each driver's underlying race pace once fuel load, tyre age, traffic and track conditions are modelled out. Each race is fitted with the parameter set its own data supports — not one fixed recipe — and every choice is shown with the reasoning behind it.

Section 01

Drivers

How far off the fastest car each driver was in every race, shown as a percentage of the fastest lap — that removes the lap-length bias that makes a tenth at Spa look like a tenth at Monaco. Switch to raw seconds below; hover any cell for both. Rows are ordered by season average, the last column.

Section 02

Teams

Team pace — the average of a team's two cars — race by race, on the same metric and the same column positions as the driver grid above, so the two read together. A × marks a race the team missed entirely.

Section 03

Fits by race

Each race is fitted with the pace-model settings its own data supports. Open a race for the fitted effects it removed from every lap — tyre wear, fuel burn, dirty air, and the rest; the full breakdown is one click further.
